How Tendons Heal

Modern sports medicine proves that tendons heal through controlled, progressive loads, not complete rest. Use a very light dumbbell (1–2 kg) for these movements. A dull pain of 2-3/10 is normal; stop if you feel a sharp jab.

1. Isometric Wrist Extension

Rest your forearm on a table, palm down, hand over the edge. Use your healthy hand to lift the weight, then hold it still without support for 45 seconds.

3-5 Reps • 45s Holds

2. Eccentric Wrist Extensions

Set up as above. Lift the weight up using your healthy hand. Remove the healthy hand, and slowly lower the weight using only your injured arm over a 4-second count.

3 Sets • 10-12 Reps

3. Hammer Twists

Grip a light dumbbell or hammer near the bottom of the handle. Slowly rotate your wrist so your palm faces all the way up, then slowly turn it until your palm faces down.

3 Sets • 10-15 Reps
